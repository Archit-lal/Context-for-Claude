Periphery

Thesis

We build software for what comes after the screen.

Every generation until now had to learn the machine. Ours is the first that won't. Technology adapts to us now, not the other way around.

When that finishes happening the screen goes. Goods and services get handled in the background, and what's left is people in rooms, talking. Every interaction that matters is with a person, and it's a conversation in some form.

We think digital only gets worse from here, and being somewhere in person only gets rarer and more valuable.

So we build for the world where you just exist. The goal is manifestation machines: intent in, reality out, nothing in between asking for your attention.
